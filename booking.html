Dinner Booking

Places at dinner are limited and must be booked in advance. The cost is £40 per person, payable by bank transfer (details on the form). Dietary requirements can be noted in the form below.

Cancellations

If you need to cancel a booking, please let us know as soon as possible by emailing the Secretary. For dinner cancellations, please note that refunds can only be offered if cancellation is received at least seven days before the event.

Payment for Dinners

On booking, you will receive an email confirming your place together with bank transfer details. Payment should be made within seven days of booking. Please use your surname and the event date as the payment reference.
