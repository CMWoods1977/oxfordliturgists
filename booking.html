Book Your Place

Lectures and seminars are free of charge; a charge applies for dinner.

Lectures & Seminars

Booking is not strictly required for lectures and seminars, but it helps us plan seating and refreshments. If you wish to attend, please complete the form below or simply arrive on the day.

Dinner Booking

Places at dinner are limited and must be booked in advance. The cost is £40 per person, payable by bank transfer (details on the form). Dietary requirements can be noted in the form below.

Cancellations

If you need to cancel a booking, please let us know as soon as possible by emailing the Convenor. For dinner cancellations, please note that refunds can only be offered if cancellation is received at least seven days before the event.

Payment for Dinners

Payment for dinner should be made on booking. If you need to contact us about this, please send us an email. Thank you.
