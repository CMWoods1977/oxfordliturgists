Welcome

The Oxford Liturgists' Network brings together clergy, academics and lay people with a shared interest in the study and practice of Christian liturgy. The Network meets regularly in Oxford for lectures, seminars and supper, drawing on a rich tradition of liturgical scholarship.

Our programme spans the breadth of liturgical enquiry — from the patristic rites of the early Church to contemporary questions of inculturation and renewal. We welcome contributions from scholars across the traditions: Anglican, Roman Catholic, Orthodox and Reformed.

What We Do

Once or twice per year, we host a series of lectures and seminars on themes in liturgical theology, history and practice. Events are usually held in central Oxford, and some are followed by dinner or drinks. Our speakers have included leading figures in liturgical scholarship from Oxford, Cambridge, Rome, and further afield.

Who We Are

The Group is open to all with a serious interest in liturgy — whether academic, pastoral or devotional. Our membership includes parish clergy, cathedral canons, university chaplains, theology students, and lay people with a love of worship in all its forms. There is no formal membership process; simply come along to an event.
