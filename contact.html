Proposing a Paper or Seminar

We are always glad to hear from those who would like to offer a lecture or lead a seminar. Please send a brief proposal (title, abstract of 150 words, and short biographical note) to the Convenor.

Finding Us

Most of our events take place at St Barnabas Jericho, just 10-15 minutes walk from the Oxford Train Station, or 10 minutes walk from Gloucester Green Bus station. Specific venues are listed on the programme page. Parking is very limited in Jericho, and we reserve Church parking for those with mobility issues.
