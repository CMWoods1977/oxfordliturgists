Joining the Mailing List

To receive termly programme cards and notices of forthcoming events, please email the Secretary with your name and preferred email address. There is no subscription fee.

Proposing a Paper or Seminar

We are always glad to hear from those who would like to offer a lecture or lead a seminar. Please send a brief proposal (title, abstract of 150 words, and short biographical note) to the Secretary. The Programme Committee meets at the beginning of each term to finalise the programme.

Finding Us

Most of our events take place in college and institutional settings in central Oxford. Specific venues are listed on the programme page. Our most frequent venue is Pusey House, located on St Giles' between the Ashmolean Museum and St John's College.

Oxford is well served by rail (Oxford station, c. 15 minutes' walk) and by bus from London, Birmingham and other cities. Limited parking is available in central Oxford; the Gloucester Green and Worcester Street car parks are closest to St Giles'.
