Saturday 17 October 2026 · 2.00 pm

Lecture

The Liturgical Theology of Alexander Schmemann: A Reassessment

The Revd Professor Andrew Louth, University of Durham

A fresh appraisal of Schmemann's sacramental vision and its continuing relevance for worship in the Western churches. How does his insistence on liturgy as the 'theology of the Church' challenge our inherited assumptions?

Saturday 7 November 2026 · 10.30 am

Seminar

The Oxford Movement and the Recovery of Liturgical Practice

Dr Sarah Foot, Regius Professor of Ecclesiastical History, University of Oxford

This seminar explores how the Tractarians reshaped Anglican worship, tracing the movement from Keble's Assize Sermon through to the ritualist controversies of the 1870s. What lessons does the Movement hold for liturgical renewal today?

Venue: Keble College, Parks Road, Oxford
